THE PLATFORM

From system surface to defensible deployment decision.

Ingest→Compile→Simulate→Attack→Replicate→Map failures→Publish / refuse

Environment factory

Turn OpenAPI, SQL, workflows, repositories, and production traces into neutral domain specifications, task families, authority boundaries, faults, and machine-checkable invariants.

Executable worlds

Compiled specifications become stateful worlds that enforce tool availability, mutation authority, injected failures, budget, and traceable consequences.

Adaptive evaluator

Measure chooses experiments by information gain per cost, expands around unknowns, stops when evidence is sufficient, and returns inconclusive when it is not.

Internal Affairs

Replay, replication, gaming detection, contamination checks, verifier certification, confidence decomposition, and constitutional publication blockers.

Private runner

Run evaluation inside customer-controlled infrastructure against HTTP or command-line participants, then upload tamper-evident evidence instead of proprietary system state.

Operational trust reports

Capability boundaries, known counterexamples, cost, reliability, failure conditions, unresolved unknowns, and the evidence behind every claim.
