PRIVACY NOTICE

Measure Privacy Notice

Effective August 16, 2026.

Information Measure processes

Measure may process account identifiers, organization and team membership, subscription and billing identifiers, API-key metadata, usage records, evaluation requests and results, support messages, production traces or other evaluation inputs a customer chooses to provide, and technical records needed to operate and secure the service.

Payments

Payment-card information is collected and processed by Stripe. Measure stores billing identifiers and subscription state needed to provide paid access, but the application is not designed to store raw card numbers.

Infrastructure providers

Measure currently uses service providers including Vercel for application hosting, Supabase for authentication and database services, and Stripe for payments. Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy practices.

Purpose

Information is used to provide and secure Measure, authenticate customers, fulfill purchases, meter usage, run requested evaluations, preserve evidence, improve reliability, provide support, prevent abuse, comply with applicable obligations, and measure whether the product's published performance claims remain supportable.

Retention

Evaluation retention depends on the customer's plan and may be shortened when required for security, contractual, or legal reasons. Billing and security records may be retained longer when reasonably necessary. Customers using the private runner can keep proprietary system execution inside their own environment and submit only the resulting evidence package.

Customer responsibilities

Do not submit secrets, raw payment credentials, or personal, regulated, confidential, or third-party data unless you are authorized to provide it and the selected Measure service is appropriate for that data. Customers are responsible for obtaining required permissions for their evaluation inputs.

Security

Measure uses access controls, hashed API keys, scoped organization records, rate limits, HTTPS deployment, tamper-evident evaluation evidence, and other controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your requests

To ask about access, correction, deletion, privacy, or another data issue, use the private Measure contact form and select Privacy. Measure will respond according to applicable requirements and the information reasonably needed to verify the request.

Changes

This notice may change as Measure evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and, where appropriate, providing additional notice.

This launch notice is operational documentation, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Enterprise agreements may include additional privacy and data-processing terms.
