# Measure Economic Role

**Category:** Strategic infrastructure with standalone enterprise potential

## Economic objective
Use Measure as trustworthy evaluation infrastructure that strengthens customer confidence across the portfolio and can also be offered to teams that need reproducible system evaluation.

## Target customer / adopter
Software and AI teams, enterprise buyers, engineering leaders, and internal portfolio projects that need reliable evaluation and repeatable evidence.

## Problem worth paying for
Teams need credible ways to compare systems and verify whether technical claims hold under reproducible conditions.

## Distribution
Technical demonstrations, portfolio integrations, enterprise engagements, developer tooling, published evaluation reports, and partnerships.

## Monetization
Enterprise evaluation engagements, hosted evaluation infrastructure, private evaluation programs, software licensing, SDK access, and indirect portfolio value when Measure strengthens the sales case for another product.

## Moat
Participant-neutral contracts, persistent world contracts, preregistration, evidence ledger, deterministic verification, replay verification, confidence decomposition, and governance mechanisms.

## Immediate economic blockers
A clear first buyer profile, packaged offer, compelling real-world evaluation program, strong report/output experience, pricing, and proof that Measure improves an actual buyer decision.

## Next highest-value action
Attach Measure to a real commercial decision: use it to support a portfolio product sale or a paid external evaluation. Build only what is required to make that decision credible and repeatable.

## Not now
Do not add evaluation worlds or mechanisms without buyer, adopter, or portfolio demand.

## Evidence required
Paid use, repeat programs, adoption, decisions influenced by Measure outputs, and portfolio deals strengthened by its evidence.
