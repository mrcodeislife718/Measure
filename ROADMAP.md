## Proof requirements before superiority claims

Measure will not claim technical superiority based on architecture alone. It must produce three external proofs:

1. **Evaluator quality** — find important system failures that incumbent/static evaluations miss.
2. **Creation economics** — demonstrate materially lower human time and cost for comparable validated coverage.
3. **Predictive validity** — show Measure results predict real deployment outcomes better than simpler benchmark scores.

Every 30x claim requires a measured denominator.
